# Ecommerce-Supply-Chain-Analysis

# Project Overview
This project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow by analyzing over 172,000 global e-commerce orders to evaluate delivery performance, profitability and supply chain efficiency. The solution integrates Python, SQL, Power BI and Machine Learning to perform data preprocessing, exploratory analysis, KPI tracking, predictive modeling and business storytelling. 
The goal is to showcase how data-driven insights can support strategic business decisions and optimize supply chain operations.

# Business Problem
The organization needed to understand the key factors affecting delivery performance, profitability and customer satisfaction. The objective was to analyze supply chain data, identify operational bottlenecks, monitor business KPIs and develop predictive insights to support data driven decision making and improve overall supply chain efficiency.

# Data Source
**Dataset:** DataCo Global Supply Chain Dataset

**Domain:** Supply Chain & E-commerce Analytics

**Source:** Public Dataset (Kaggle) , https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/jolenechen/datacosupplychaindataset

**Dataset Size:** 172,000+ Global Order Records

**Key Attributes:**
- Customer Information
- Product Details
- Order & Sales Data
- Shipping Information
- Delivery Status
- Profit & Revenue Metrics
- Geographic Information

# Tech Stack
- Python (Pandas, NumPy) – Data cleaning, preprocessing, feature engineering and exploratory data analysis (EDA).
- SQL – Data querying, transformation, aggregation and KPI development.
- Scikit-learn – Built a Random Forest model to predict delivery delays.
- Matplotlib & Seaborn – Created analytical visualizations for trend and pattern analysis.
- Power BI – Developed interactive dashboards for monitoring operational KPIs and business performance.
- Google Collab Notebook – Conducted and documented the end-to-end analytics workflow.

# Highlights
- Analyzed 172K+ Global E-commerce Orders to uncover delivery trends, profitability insights and supply chain performance.
- Performed End-to-End Data Preparation, including data cleaning, preprocessing, feature engineering and exploratory data analysis (EDA).
- Developed Business KPIs to monitor delivery performance, shipping efficiency, customer behavior and financial metrics.
- Conducted Root Cause Analysis to identify factors contributing to delayed deliveries and operational inefficiencies.
- Built a Random Forest Predictive Model achieving 74% accuracy to identify high-risk deliveries.
- Designed Interactive Power BI Dashboards with KPI cards, drill-through analysis, filters and business-focused visualizations.
- Generated Actionable Business Recommendations to improve delivery performance, reduce operational risks and support data-driven decision-making.

# Business Impact
